# Weddings, proposals and festivals

## Wedding flights

Our Wedding Flight costs 450 gold. You get two Ridgebacks with ribbons on
their horns, a keeper on the ground, and a flight from the ceremony to the
celebration at any of our 12 landing fields. Gerald is the most requested
wedding dragon. Book at least 30 days ahead.

At a wedding, keep the rings in a closed box in a pocket, never in your hand.
Dragons hoard shiny things, and a wedding ring is the shiniest thing most
dragons will ever see. Ask your guests not to throw rice: dragons try to catch
it, and they can't.

## Proposal flights

A proposal flight is a sunset flight on a Ridgeback for two, to Brightwater
Shore and back, for 60 gold. The keeper holds the ring until you land and
hands it to you out of the dragon's sight. Do not propose in the air. Many
riders have tried, and the ring is now in a hoard.

## Birthday parties

Birthday parties at the roost cost 8 gold per child, for at least 6 children.
Every child meets the hatchlings Pip and Kettle and has a ride around the
paddock on Dumpling, with a keeper leading. Party food is up to you, but goat
isn't allowed, at any time of day.

## Festival days

Thunderwings are usually gone by mid-morning on festival days, so book ahead
by raven if you can. On Lantern Festival night, only Whisper flies, because
Moth chases the lanterns and the other dragons try to eat them. Lantern Lane
Field is closed that night.

## Dragon Day

Dragon Day is our open day at the roost, held every year at midsummer. Entry
is free. Visitors can meet every dragon, watch the keepers oil the scales, and
take a free short hop on Dumpling. The queue for Dumpling is long, and Dumpling
does not hurry it.

## The Great Moult

The roost is closed during the Great Moult in early spring, when every dragon
sheds its scales and nobody is in the mood. It lasts about two weeks. We
don't take hires or visitors, and ravens sent during the Moult are answered
when it's over.

Moulted scales are cleaned and sold in the roost shop afterwards, for 2 gold
each. A Thunderwing scale makes a good shield for a child and a poor one for
an adult.
