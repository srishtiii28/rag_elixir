# Working at the roost

## Keepers

Ember & Scale has ten keepers: Nesta Ashryver, the head keeper; Fen, who runs
the riding school; Tobias, who leads the rescue riders; Maud, who runs
bookings; and six more keepers who feed, oil and look after the dragons and
give the safety briefings.

## Becoming a keeper

Keepers start as apprentices. An apprentice must be at least 16, must not be
afraid of heights or smoke, and must get on with every kind of dragon, which
the dragons decide. An apprenticeship takes three years, and the first year
is mostly carrying turnips.

To ask about an apprenticeship, send a raven to Nesta Ashryver. We take on
new apprentices once a year, after the Great Moult, and only when a dragon
has chosen them. Being chosen usually means the dragon sits next to you and
won't move.

## Rescue riders

The rescue riders answer red flares. They are led by Tobias, who flies Hazel
and carries a healer's kit, and they usually reach a rider who lights a flare
within 45 minutes. Rescue riders are experienced keepers; we don't take
volunteers for rescue work.

## Volunteering

Volunteers help on Dragon Day, run the queue for Dumpling's free short hops,
and help the keepers at the viewing wall. Volunteers don't handle the dragons
without a keeper and don't fly. Send a raven to Maud if you would like to
volunteer.
