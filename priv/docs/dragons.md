# Hiring a dragon

Ember & Scale Dragon Hire keeps 23 dragons at our roost on Cinder Hill, just
past the old windmill. Twenty-one of them can be hired. The other two are
hatchlings, who are far too small to ride and far too pleased with themselves
to care.

We open from dawn until the second bell after sunset, every day except during
the Great Moult in early spring, when every dragon sheds its scales and nobody
is in the mood.

We hire out four kinds of dragon: Puddlewyrms, Ridgebacks, Thunderwings and
Mistgliders. We don't sell dragons, of any kind, at any price. See "Buying a
dragon" for why, and for what you can do instead.

## Puddlewyrms

Puddlewyrms are our smallest dragons, about the size of a carthorse. They fly
low, slowly and very politely, and they are the best choice for first-time
riders. A Puddlewyrm costs 12 gold an hour or 60 gold for a full day.

A Puddlewyrm carries one rider. Puddlewyrms cruise at about 15 miles an hour,
never fly higher than a church spire, and stop to look at ducks. We keep 8 of
them. They are the only dragons children may ride with a parent walking
alongside on the ground.

Puddlewyrms are nervous in rain and stay on the ground in any of it. They are
brave about almost everything else, except pigeons, loud sneezes and one
particular scarecrow on the Brightwater road.

## Ridgebacks

Ridgebacks are our all-rounders. They carry two riders, cruise at about 40
miles an hour and don't mind light rain. A Ridgeback costs 25 gold an hour or
120 gold for a full day.

A Ridgeback can carry two riders plus 40 pounds of luggage in saddlebags. We
keep 7 Ridgebacks. They are steady, sensible and easy to steer, and they are
our most popular dragon for day trips, weddings and anyone who wants to get
somewhere rather than just go up and come down again.

Ridgebacks are the only dragons that can swim. They will happily land in
Brightwater Lake on a hot day whether you asked them to or not, so wear
something you don't mind getting wet.

## Thunderwings

Thunderwings are big, fast and dramatic. They carry up to four riders and can
cross the Grey Mountains in an afternoon. A Thunderwing costs 60 gold an hour
or 300 gold for a full day, and you need our Advanced Rider badge to hire one.

Thunderwings are our fastest dragons: a Thunderwing cruises at about 90 miles
an hour and climbs faster than any other dragon we keep. We have 4
Thunderwings. Riders must be at least 18 to hire a Thunderwing, and every
rider on a Thunderwing must wear a helmet, whatever their age.

Thunderwings like to arrive. They will circle a landing field twice so
everyone can see them, and they roar on landing. This is normal. It is not a
sign that anything is wrong, and there is no way to stop it.

## Mistgliders

Mistgliders are our night dragons: pale grey, almost silent, and very hard to
see against the sky. A Mistglider carries one rider and is only hired after
sunset. A Mistglider costs 35 gold an hour, or 120 gold for a whole night.

Mistglider hires start at sunset and end at the midnight bell, which is the
only exception to our rule that dragons are back by the second bell after
sunset. We keep 2 Mistgliders. You need our Night Flying certificate to hire
one, which you can earn in an evening lesson for 25 gold.

Mistgliders don't like lanterns, music or being looked at directly. They fly
slowly, at about 20 miles an hour, and are the best way to see the stars, the
town lights and the glow-moths over Brightwater Lake.

## Which dragon should I choose?

For a first flight, a nervous rider or a gentle look around, choose a
Puddlewyrm. For two people, a day trip or most weather, choose a Ridgeback.
For speed, the mountains or a group of up to four experienced riders, choose a
Thunderwing. For a quiet flight under the stars, choose a Mistglider.

If you can't decide, tell us at the roost what you want to do. The keepers
will match you to a dragon, and the dragon will have an opinion too.

## Tall or heavy riders

Thunderwings are our strongest dragons, and the best choice for a tall or heavy
rider: a Thunderwing carries four riders without noticing. A Ridgeback carries
two riders, so one large rider is no trouble for it either.

Puddlewyrms and Mistgliders are small and carry one rider each. We don't put
very tall or heavy riders on a Puddlewyrm, because it will try its best and then
sit down in a field. If you're not sure, ask a keeper at the roost.

## What's included

Every hire includes a saddle, a riding helmet with goggles, a fireproof cloak
and a bag of 20 dragon treats. Saddlebags cost 3 gold a day. We also lend you
a whistle: one long blast tells most dragons to land.

Every hire also includes the free map of the 12 marked landing fields, and a
red flare in the saddlebag for emergencies. Fire Cover is not included; it
costs 15 gold a day and must be added when you book.

## Booking

You can book by raven up to 60 days ahead, or just walk in. Thunderwings are
usually gone by mid-morning on festival days. Group hires of 6 or more dragons
need 14 days' notice, because the dragons need time to agree on who flies at
the front.

To book by raven, send one to the roost with your name, the dragon you want,
the date and how long you want it for. Maud, who runs bookings, replies by
return raven within a day. Please don't send the raven with gold; ravens
spend it.

You can't choose a particular dragon by name when you book, but you can ask,
and we will try. Regular riders often have a favourite. The dragons have
favourites too, and these are not always the same people.
