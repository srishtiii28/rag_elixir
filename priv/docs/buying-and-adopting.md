# Buying a dragon

You can't buy a dragon from us. We don't sell dragons: not Puddlewyrms, not
Ridgebacks, not Thunderwings, not Mistgliders, and not the hatchlings, for any
amount of gold. The dragons at Ember & Scale live at the roost, and we only
ever hire them out.

There are two reasons we don't sell dragons. The first is the town council's
Dragon Charter, which says a dragon can't be owned by a person, only looked
after. The second is that the dragons have never agreed to it, and a dragon
that hasn't agreed to something does not stay sold for long.

If you want a dragon for a long time, the longest hire we allow is 28 days.
Hires of 3 days or more get 20% off the daily price, and a full week on a
Ridgeback costs 600 gold flat.

## Dragon eggs

We never sell dragon eggs, and we don't know of anyone who honestly does. If
someone offers you a dragon egg, it is almost certainly a painted rock. If it
isn't a painted rock, it is a very serious matter: please don't buy it, and
tell us at the roost where you saw it.

## Sponsoring a dragon

You can't adopt a dragon and take it home, but you can sponsor one. Sponsoring
costs 5 gold a month, and you can choose any of our dragons, including the
hatchlings Pip and Kettle and our retired dragon, Grandmother Ash.

Every sponsor gets a letter from their dragon each season, written by a keeper
and signed with a scorched paw print. Sponsors can visit their dragon at the
roost once a season, and sponsors of Grandmother Ash are the only visitors
allowed into the old barn to see her.

Sponsoring doesn't give you a discount on hires or first choice of dragon. It
pays for feed, scale oil, and the roof of the old barn, which the Thunderwings
keep sitting on.

## Retired dragons

When a dragon at Ember & Scale is too old to fly for hire, it retires to the
roost and stays with us for the rest of its life. Retired dragons are never
sold or given away. At the moment we have one retired dragon, Grandmother Ash.
