# Getting along with your dragon

## Moods and signs

A dragon that is humming is happy. A dragon with smoke curling from its left
nostril is content. Smoke from both nostrils means it's grumpy, and smoke from
both nostrils for more than an hour means it's unwell: land and light your
flare.

A dragon that thumps its tail once is saying yes. Thumping it three times
means it has had enough of whatever you are doing. A dragon that lies flat and
puts its chin on the ground is sulking, and will stay there until it is
apologised to.

## Things dragons like

Dragons like being called by their name, being told they are handsome, warm
stones, the treats in your bag, and being scratched behind the horns. Every
dragon at Ember & Scale has a favourite spot to be scratched, and the keepers
will tell you where it is.

Dragons like being asked rather than told. "Shall we land here?" works better
than a whistle, though the whistle works too.

## Things dragons don't like

Never call a dragon a lizard. Dragons also dislike umbrellas, bagpipes, cats,
being laughed at, and anyone mentioning the Duke's trebuchet. Don't open an
umbrella on a dragon: it thinks it's a wing and tries to fight it.

Don't whistle a tune near a dragon. Dragons can't tell a tune from the landing
signal, and you will land wherever you happen to be.

## When your dragon sulks

If your dragon sulks, get off, stand where it can see you, say its name, tell
it it's doing very well, and offer one treat. This works on every dragon at
the roost except Lady Pemberton, who also expects you to use her full name,
and Brick, who sulks for about ten minutes whatever you do.

## Shiny things

Dragons hoard anything shiny: keys, rings, buckles, spoons, spectacles and
coins. They don't steal so much as decide that a shiny thing has always been
theirs. Keep shiny things in a closed saddlebag. If your dragon takes one, we
recover it from the hoard within 3 days, free of charge.
