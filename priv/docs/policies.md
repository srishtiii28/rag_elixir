# Policies

## Deposits

Every hire needs a deposit and proof of who you are. The deposit is 100 gold
for a Puddlewyrm, 250 gold for a Ridgeback, 400 gold for a Mistglider and 800
gold for a Thunderwing. We return it within 5 days of the dragon coming home in
a good mood. We don't accept deposits in livestock.

## Paying

We take gold and silver at the roost. Silver is taken at the rate posted on
the board by the door that morning. We also accept Guild bank notes sent by
raven. We don't accept livestock, jewellery, promises, or anything the dragon
has already hoarded.

## Fire damage

Small scorch marks on your clothes, hair or picnic are normal and never
charged. Anything your dragon sets on fire while you are riding it is your
responsibility, and repairs are taken from the deposit first. Fire Cover costs
15 gold a day when you book and covers fire damage up to 2,000 gold. Without
Fire Cover, you pay the full cost of the damage.

Fire Cover has to be added when you book; it can't be added once the hire has
started. A dragon sneeze counts as fire. Fire Cover doesn't cover anything set
on fire on purpose, such as a bonfire you asked your dragon to light.

## Cancellations

You can cancel for free up to 48 hours before your hire starts. Between 48 and
24 hours before, we keep half the price. With less than 24 hours' notice we
keep the full price, because the dragon has already been told and is excited.
If we cancel because of a storm warning, you get a full refund or a free new
date.

## Late returns

Dragons must be back at the roost by the second bell after sunset. Late
returns cost 10 gold an hour, up to the price of one extra day. If a dragon is
more than 24 hours late and we haven't heard from you, we send out the search
griffins, and the cost of the search (usually 150 gold) comes out of your
deposit.

Mistgliders must be back by the midnight bell instead, and the same late fees
apply from then. If you are going to be late, send a raven or blow three short
blasts on your whistle: your dragon knows the way home even when you don't.

## Who can ride

Riders must be at least 16 to hire a dragon on their own and at least 18 to
hire a Thunderwing. Under-16s can ride with a parent or guardian who signs the
hire agreement. Helmets are compulsory for riders under 18 and strongly
recommended for everyone else.

Children under 8 can ride only a Puddlewyrm, and only with a parent walking
alongside on the ground. Every rider on a Thunderwing must wear a helmet,
whatever their age.

You don't need a licence to hire a Puddlewyrm or a Ridgeback: every rider gets
a 10-minute safety briefing at the roost before take-off. Thunderwings need our
Advanced Rider badge, which you can earn in a half-day lesson for 40 gold.
Mistgliders need our Night Flying certificate, from an evening lesson for 25
gold.

We don't hire dragons to anyone who has been drinking. The dragons can smell
it, and they don't think it's funny either.

## Riders with disabilities

Riders who can't climb can use the mounting ramp, and riders who can't grip
can use our harness saddle, which holds you in place. Both are free; please
mention them when you book so a keeper can fit the saddle before you arrive.
Puddlewyrms and Ridgebacks are best for the harness saddle.

## Lost and hoarded items

Dragons hoard shiny things. If yours takes your keys, rings or spectacles,
don't try to get them back mid-flight. Tell us when you return and we will
recover them from its hoard within 3 days, free of charge. We are not
responsible for anything a dragon swallows.

If you lose something that isn't shiny, we look for it in the saddlebags and
on the landing field where you last landed. Dragons don't hoard dull things,
but they do sometimes sit on them.

## Complaints

If something went wrong, tell the keeper when you return, or send a raven to
Nesta Ashryver, our head keeper. We reply within a week. Complaints about a
dragon's attitude are passed on to the dragon, who will not reply.
