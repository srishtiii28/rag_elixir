# Riding school

The riding school at Ember & Scale is run by Fen, our riding instructor, from
the paddock behind the roost. Lessons are taught on Dumpling, Lady Pemberton,
Hazel, Saffron and Whisper. Book lessons the same way as hires, by raven or
at the roost.

## First flight lesson

The first flight lesson is a one-hour lesson on a Puddlewyrm for 15 gold. It
covers mounting, steering, the whistle signals and landing, and ends with a
short flight around Cinder Hill. You don't need a lesson to hire a Puddlewyrm
or a Ridgeback, but nervous riders tell us it helps.

## Advanced Rider badge

You need the Advanced Rider badge to hire a Thunderwing. It takes a half-day
lesson that costs 40 gold. You must be at least 18, and you must have flown a
Ridgeback at least once before. The lesson covers fast climbs, turning at
speed, carrying passengers and landing a dragon that would rather show off.

At the end of the Advanced Rider lesson you fly Saffron once around the Grey
Mountains foothills with Fen alongside. If Fen is happy, you get the badge. If
not, you can take the flight again another day for 10 gold. The badge never
expires, but riders who haven't flown a Thunderwing for two years are asked to
fly with Fen once before hiring.

## Night Flying certificate

You need the Night Flying certificate to hire a Mistglider. It takes one
evening lesson, costs 25 gold, and is taught on Whisper. It covers flying by
starlight, finding the landing fields in the dark, and the rule about not
looking a Mistglider in the eye.

## Lessons for children

Children aged 8 to 15 can take the Young Riders course: four lessons on
Puddlewyrms over four weeks, for 40 gold, with a parent or guardian signing
the hire agreement. Young Riders who finish the course get a wooden dragon
pin, which they are very proud of, and a certificate, which they lose.
