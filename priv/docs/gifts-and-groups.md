# Gifts, groups and school visits

## Gift vouchers

Ember & Scale gift vouchers can be bought at the roost or by raven, for any
amount from 10 gold. A voucher is valid for one year and can be used for any
hire, lesson, Wedding Flight, proposal flight or birthday party. Vouchers
can't be exchanged for gold, and the dragons can't be persuaded to accept
them as treats.

A voucher doesn't pay the deposit. Whoever uses the voucher still needs proof
of who they are and the deposit for their dragon on the day, which we return
as usual within 5 days of the dragon coming home in a good mood.

The most popular gift is the first flight lesson, a one-hour lesson on a
Puddlewyrm for 15 gold. A sponsorship, 5 gold a month for any dragon, is a
good gift for someone who would rather not leave the ground.

## Group hires

Group hires of 6 or more dragons need 14 days' notice, because the dragons
need time to agree on who flies at the front. Groups don't get a discount on
the hourly or daily price, but hires of 3 days or more get 20% off the daily
price, for groups as for anyone else.

Every rider in a group needs their own safety briefing, and every dragon
needs its own deposit. A group flying together must keep three dragon lengths
apart, which means a group of six dragons takes up more sky than people
expect.

## School visits

School groups of 10 or more children should book their visit ahead by raven,
even though visiting the roost is free. A keeper gives the group a talk at
the viewing wall, the children meet the hatchlings Pip and Kettle at feeding
time, and everyone eats at the Singed Kettle if the school wants to.

On a school visit nobody flies, but children aged 8 to 15 can sign up for the
Young Riders course afterwards: four lessons on Puddlewyrms over four weeks,
for 40 gold, with a parent or guardian signing the hire agreement.
