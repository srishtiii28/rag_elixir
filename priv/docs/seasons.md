# The roost through the year

## Spring

Spring starts with the Great Moult, when every dragon sheds its scales and
the roost closes for about two weeks. We don't take hires or visitors during
the Moult, and ravens are answered when it's over. Moulted scales go on sale
in the roost shop afterwards, for 2 gold each.

After the Moult the dragons are glossy, hungry and keen to fly. Spring showers
keep Puddlewyrms on the ground more than any other season, so book a
Ridgeback if you can't move your date. Pepper sneezes most in spring, and a
dragon sneeze counts as fire.

## Summer

Summer has the longest days, and since every dragon except the Mistgliders is
back by the second bell after sunset, a summer full-day hire is the longest
flying day of the year. Mistglider nights are short in summer, but the
glow-moths over Brightwater Lake are at their brightest in late summer.

Dragon Day, our free open day, is held every year at midsummer. Visitors can
meet every dragon, watch the keepers oil the scales, and take a free short hop
on Dumpling. On hot days the Ridgebacks land in Brightwater Lake whenever they
get the chance.

## Autumn

Autumn is harvest time. Farmers are busy and fields are full, so keep to the
12 marked landing fields: landing anywhere else earns a 20 gold fine from the
town council, and flattened crops are paid for on top. The sea wind on the
Coast Run gets stronger as the autumn goes on.

The Lantern Festival is held in autumn. On Lantern Festival night only
Whisper flies, because Moth chases the lanterns and the other dragons try to
eat them, and Lantern Lane Field is closed. Thunderwings are usually gone by
mid-morning on every festival day.

## Winter

Winter days are short, so day hires are short too, and the Mistgliders have
their longest nights. Snow is fine for every dragon except Puddlewyrms, who
try to eat it. Dragons don't mind the cold, but riders do: wear more layers
than you think you need.

The roost stays open all winter, from dawn until the second bell after
sunset. The dragons sleep closer together in the long barn when it is cold,
and the Singed Kettle is the warmest café in the county, thanks to Crumpet
asleep under the floor.
