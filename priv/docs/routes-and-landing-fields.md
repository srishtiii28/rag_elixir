# Where to fly

Every hire comes with the free map of our 12 marked landing fields. Landing
anywhere else earns a 20 gold fine from the town council. Flight times below
are on a Ridgeback; a Puddlewyrm takes about three times as long, and a
Thunderwing about half.

## The 12 landing fields

The 12 marked landing fields are: Cinder Hill (the roost itself), Windmill
Meadow, Brightwater Shore, Oldbridge Green, Saltmarsh Point, the Abbey Ruins,
Hollow Oak Clearing, Fairmarket Common, Lantern Lane Field, Shepherd's Top,
Greystone Pass and the Far Beacon.

Greystone Pass and the Far Beacon are in the Grey Mountains and are really only
reachable on a Thunderwing. Lantern Lane Field is closed on Lantern Festival
nights. Shepherd's Top has a great many sheep, and Gerald will want to stay.

## Popular routes

The Lake Loop goes from Cinder Hill to Brightwater Shore and back over the
water, about 40 minutes each way on a Ridgeback. It is the best route for a
first proper flight. Ridgebacks may land in the lake; Puddlewyrms will not go
near it.

The Coast Run follows the cliffs to Saltmarsh Point, about an hour and a half
on a Ridgeback. Watch out for gulls, which Toast thinks are pigeons, and for
the sea wind, which can be strong in the afternoon.

The Mountain Crossing goes from Cinder Hill over the Grey Mountains to the Far
Beacon. A Thunderwing does it in an afternoon. It is the only route that needs
a Thunderwing, and the only one where you must carry the red flare in a pocket
rather than the saddlebag.

The Starlight Glide is our night route for Mistgliders, from Cinder Hill to
Brightwater Shore to watch the glow-moths over the lake, then back by the
midnight bell.

## Places to avoid

Never fly over the royal palace, the cathedral or the Duke's orchards. The
Duke's orchards lie between Oldbridge Green and Fairmarket Common, so go round
them to the north. The Duke has a trebuchet and is not afraid to use it.

Keep away from the scarecrow on the Brightwater road if you are on a
Puddlewyrm. Nobody knows what is wrong with it, but every Puddlewyrm agrees
that something is.

## Picnics

Brightwater Shore, Hollow Oak Clearing and the Abbey Ruins are the best
landing fields for a picnic. Sit upwind of your dragon. Small scorch marks on
your picnic are normal and never charged, and the dragon will expect a share,
which it may not have.
