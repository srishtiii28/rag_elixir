# Your first hire, step by step

## Before the day

Book by raven up to 60 days ahead, or just walk in. If you want a Thunderwing,
a Mistglider or a particular dragon by name, book ahead. Add Fire Cover when
you book if you want it: it costs 15 gold a day and can't be added once the
hire has started.

On the morning of your hire, check the sky. Puddlewyrms stay on the ground in
any rain, and no dragon flies in a storm. If we cancel because of a storm
warning, you get a full refund or a free new date, and Maud will send a raven
to tell you.

## What to bring

Bring proof of who you are and your deposit: 100 gold for a Puddlewyrm, 250
gold for a Ridgeback, 400 gold for a Mistglider or 800 gold for a Thunderwing.
We take gold, silver at the morning's posted rate, and Guild bank notes.

Wear warm clothes, even in summer, because it is always colder in the air.
Wear boots or closed shoes, tie back long hair and leave scarves at home: a
dragon will think a loose scarf is a game. Riding gloves are sold in the roost
shop if you forget yours.

Don't bring an umbrella, a cat, bagpipes or anything shiny in an open bag.
Dragons fight umbrellas, have an old disagreement with cats, dislike bagpipes,
and hoard anything shiny they can reach.

## When you arrive

Arrive half an hour before your hire starts. Stable any horse at the Windmill
Inn, a mile down the hill, and walk up. Maud checks your booking, takes your
deposit and the hire price, and gives you the free map of the 12 marked
landing fields.

A keeper then gives you the 10-minute safety briefing, checks your helmet,
goggles and saddle straps, and makes sure the whistle and red flare are in
your saddlebag. Finally the keeper introduces you to your dragon by name and
tells you where it likes to be scratched.

## During the hire

Stay below the clouds, keep to the marked landing fields, and keep three
dragon lengths from other dragons. Give treats after landing, never in the
air. If anything goes wrong, one long blast on the whistle tells most dragons
to land, and the red flare brings the rescue riders.

## Coming back

Bring your dragon back by the second bell after sunset, or by the midnight
bell on a Mistglider. A keeper checks the dragon over, takes back the helmet,
cloak and whistle, and asks how the flight went. The dragon is asked too.

Your deposit is returned within 5 days of the dragon coming home in a good
mood. If your dragon took anything shiny of yours, tell the keeper now: we
recover it from the hoard within 3 days, free of charge.
